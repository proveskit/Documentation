# Chapter 3: Power Stop
In this chapter, the user will learn how to properly assembly the Power Stop. These go on four of the eight feet on the satellite and turn the satellite off when it is not deployed

Repeat these steps four times to make four power stops:

1. Cut 7mm of black and 7mm red heat shrink
2. Insert heat shrink into the power stop harness and slide all the way to the end 
3. Apply flux to wire tips and power button ends

*<p align="center">**Figure 3.1:**</p>*
![Figure 3-1](images/3.1applyflux.png)

*<p align="center">**Figure 3.2:**</p>*
![Figure 3-2](images/3.2applyflux.png)

4. Apply solder to wire tips and power button ends ends
5. Re-apply flux to wire tip 
6. C is black wire 
7. CN is red wire
8. Solder the red wire to the pin labeled CN on the button
9. Solder the black wire to the pin labeled C on the button

*<p align="center">**Figure 3.3:**</p>*
![Figure 3-3](images/3.3solderedfoot.png.png)

10. Clean tips with alcohol to remove flux, so that flux does not corrode metal. Then push shrink wrap onto the soldered ends, and shrink with a heat gun.

*<p align="center">**Figure 3.4:**</p>*
![Figure 3-4](images/3.4shrinkwrapped.png)

11. Insert pusher or screw into power stop harness casing. 

*<p align="center">**Figure 3.5:**</p>*
![Figure 3-5](images/3.5insertinpusher.png)

12. Assemble power stop harness into casing and glue tight. Use space level glue or epoxy. Follow the instructions on your glue. 


*<p align="center">**Figure 3.6:**</p>*
![Figure 3-6](images/3.6pusherinside.png)

13. Test the power stops by plugging them into the FC board and clicking them. Check the light on the FC board blinks. This means the signal is going through

14. Screw harness onto CubeSat case. It should not be possible to place the cases incorrectly, as if they are flush against the wall, they can only line up with their respective holes. Add nylocks, use the hex screws 1.5 allen
